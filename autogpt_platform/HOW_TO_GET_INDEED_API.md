# How to Get Indeed Publisher ID - Step by Step with Pictures

## 🎯 Super Simple Guide (2 Minutes)

Don't worry! Getting the Publisher ID is very easy. Just follow these steps:

---

## Step 1: Go to Indeed Publisher Page

Open your web browser and go to:

**https://www.indeed.com/publisher**

You'll see a page like this:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Indeed Publisher                       │
│                                         │
│  [Sign In]  or  [Register]             │
│                                         │
│  Monetize your traffic with Indeed     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## Step 2: Sign In

Click the **"Sign In"** button at the top right.

Use your **existing Indeed account**:
- Email: your_indeed_email@example.com
- Password: your_indeed_password

If you don't have an Indeed account yet:
1. Click "Create Account"
2. Fill in: Name, Email, Password
3. Click "Create Account"

---

## Step 3: Register as Publisher

After signing in, you'll see a registration form. Fill it in:

### Required Information:

1. **First Name**: Your first name
2. **Last Name**: Your last name
3. **Email**: your_email@example.com (auto-filled)
4. **Company/Website**: 
   - You can write: "Personal Use" 
   - Or: "http://localhost:3000"
5. **How will you use Indeed?**: 
   - Select: "Job Board" or "Personal Project"
6. **Country**: Select "United Arab Emirates"

### Click "Submit" or "Register"

---

## Step 4: Get Your Publisher ID

After submitting, you'll see your **Publisher Dashboard**.

Look for a number that looks like this:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Your Publisher ID: 1234567890123      │
│                                         │
│  Copy this number!                      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**That's your Publisher ID!**

It's usually a **10-15 digit number**.

---

## Step 5: Use in Dashboard

Now go back to the Job Hunt Dashboard:

1. Open: **http://localhost:3000/job-hunt**
2. Click the **"Platforms"** tab
3. Find the **"Indeed"** section
4. Paste your Publisher ID in the box
5. Click **"Connect"**

You'll see a green **"✓ Connected"** badge!

---

## 🎉 Done!

You can now search for jobs in Dubai using Indeed!

Go to **"Job Search"** tab and click **"Search for Jobs"**.

---

## ⚠️ Common Problems

### "I can't find my Publisher ID"

After registering, it should show immediately. If you don't see it:

1. Look for "Account Settings" or "Dashboard"
2. Look for "API" or "Publisher ID"
3. It's usually on the main page after login

### "Registration is asking for too much information"

You can use simple answers:
- Website: "localhost" or "personal use"
- Company: Your name or "Individual"
- Purpose: "Job Search Automation"

### "It says Publisher program not available in UAE"

If this happens, try:
- Use VPN to a supported country (US, UK, Canada)
- Or use Adzuna instead (also free, works in UAE)
